Data is fraught with risk. One can see patterns in graphs and charts and agree that they exist but it is another thing to insist that they are systemically relevant and meaningful. Correlation famously is not always causation and one will tend to see what one wishes to. As well an analogy may be applied in the real world only so far and we are prone to take ideas, especially those steeped in mythology literally so that we argue veracity, fidelity, nomenclature, dimensions and geography. There are too, the distractions of sophistry and argumentation for its own sake presented to bolster reputation, standing and ego; the ruse of the clever halo effect is mistaken by the earnestly naive and suggestible for competent rhetoric. Finally, the mythology of jurisdiction in particular is prone to flights of emotion as loyalty and entrenched prejudices tear at our objectivity in analysing evidence.                                           

Still, data need not be precise or complete to enable prudent behaviour as long as we understand the bare principles of how value and meaning are constituted. To illustrate take one enduring myth which applies to the idea of capital or value in its various forms as expressed in the chart above. Specifically the story of Noah who as a Janus caretaker or custodian carries value constituted as truth and beauty (or at least that which discerns it as into the distant horizon of the future with the utility of a special purpose vehicle - the Ark.

Noah looks around and sees civilisation in the throes of heady bacchanalian consumption; humanity in an orgy of waste ravages the earth in a never ending feast which knows no limits. One without *rationalism* which is to say without reserve or any sense of proportion nor any notion of a fair share; what we would normally refer to as rationing has become something which is done in a when things are scarce. While people know of course that the planet is finite and scarce they feel helpless to stop its exploitation and instead a sense of hopeless profligacy envelops the world. Noah in response assesses and claims a *ratio* of material from the commonwealth's store; no more nor less than that which is required to sustain Creation for posterity. Only that which is required to *yield* salvation. 

So when we speak of maintaining systemic balance, stability or sustainability all we mean is that there must be some adjustment to ensure *equivalence* into the future of our world's present value. This symmetry or elegance we refer to as beauty or aesthetic quality. Ease, quietude, flow are all related ideas. How might we then encourage the occupants of a jurisdiction *as a whole* to take custody and guardianship of the domain or as it is sometimes called the commons? Firstly by affording them an opportunity to bring their particular gift to humanity - the innate talent that they would express which in some way benefits the domain because they find pleasure in *flow* versus that which is found in purely sensory gratification. Secondly the ability to set aside a surplus in notional terms of the social credit earned as they develop and express that talent. The outcome, beauty, may be seen as an elegant outcome of flow resulting in ease; flour milled with  the turning of a wheel hitched to the wind instead of the excessive toil of *brute* force wear and tear of sinew and muscle.

On that note let's dial the unit of analysis to the semantic perimeter of an individual identity instead of the domain. Just as Noah needs to take stock of the inventory and account for the entirety of creation (and the primal truth it represents) as he seeks to preserve and extend its value, so too does an individual only fully express the full truth and primal reality of their existence or self-actualisation by identifying their native attributes. In juxtaposing that primal truth against the stark reality of the world replete with its problems that require solutions (a deep gauge of beauty beheld or the lack thereof) their Calling then amounts to utility and the ratio to which they may subjectively extend themselves and their Calling towards resolving a problem in the real world. To be sovereign or a sovereign amounts to taking account of how one takes responsibility to govern and wrestle with the aesthetic challenges confronted in the world - or at least the perimeter within which he sees actionable opportunity. 

Key defines what is internal (private) versus what is external (public) in reference to a given semantic perimeter of identity embodied (*semper idem*). This is true regardless of one's reference (individual, community, corporation, nation-state, etc.). The node is the place where an identity *meets* or comes together with its peers in an ecosystem and the network they constitute together. It amounts to a negotiated metric of status (relevance, credibility and so forth). You can think  of this as a place where discussion happens to derive and assign 'ownership' for an interaction in the commons (in every sense). This is where the social contract is defined or verified and where provenance is established and the connection is made between identity and an impression made by it as represented by the public key. Such representations may be minimal, as in a proof of location or coordinates delimiting a broadly recognised perimeter, or intellectually substantive, as in the use of a shibboleth or argot (a lexicon or glossary) as a proof of work. Accordingly, a private key references identity itself, while its joined or corresponding public key references the imprint attributable to that identity within the public domain. Leaving aside for the moment the matter of how fairly or evenly wealth or prosperity is distributed within a domain (in mythical terms this is providence),  we place the interests of those who are within our semantic perimeter however defined (home, community, region, etc) ahead of those outside it.

Think of a society, not as complex as ours but still orderly and structured. Those of our indigenous communities. Or the first societies which formed; ones with *implicit* rules rather than the explicit ones we have today (i.e. law). Obligations, except for the very young *always* precede rights. For example you cannot claim a *rightful* share of gathered and hunted victual without first having participated and asserted *some* talent to express the feeling that you want to belong to a community. Invariably you will introspect on what that may be as you scan your immediate surroundings to find a gap that needs to be filled; a reality or truth which needs to be adjusted to align better with what your nascent community requires as it seeks comfort. In economy, you are *obligated* to stake an investment in order to affect a *return* on investment. In our health and welfare we are obligated to invest time, effort and care in relative youth to reap the return of a relatively comfortable old age.

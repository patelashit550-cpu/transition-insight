## Breathwork

Deliberate regulation of breath as the hinge between autonomic life and conscious agency. In this corpus: the practice that inserts calm where panic is, restores receptivity, and precedes contemplation — the first move of the Yoga Element taken off the mat into civic life is to be with that breath as you connect and flow with what is in front you.

## Dharana

Sanskrit धारणा: focused attention or concentration. Bringing the whole self to a scene until immersion replaces distraction. Here: the discipline that makes storytelling and encounter formative rather than merely spectacular — and that lets a person write their own narrative with each interaction.

## Dhyana

Sanskrit ध्यान: meditation — sustained attention beyond the initial grip of concentration. Follows *dharana*; precedes *samadhi*. Here: quietude in which insights gathered from the world are integrated into a model before one returns to act.

## Dollar Regime

The broad semantic and institutional perimeter organized around the US dollar — Federal Reserve at its heart, with sovereigns maintaining shared financial plumbing and global bodies (IMF, World Bank) expected to balance regional interests. Here: the architecture under examination for commitment, credibility, and fitness for a resilient Transition. The contention has been the weaponization of a *national* currency for state interests. This has long been a matter of concern for both ordinary people and commentators in the United States and elsewhere.

## Equivalence

Recognition of equal standing without insisting on identity. Distinct from equality-as-sameness: you are there and I am here, and that difference of place is already a difference of perspective. Discussion as *mudra* aims at equivalence.

## Lingam

In Yoga tradition: the mark or emblem of creative force; often read as the generative axis. Ling (language) adheres to am (identity) to define a semantic reference with *systemic* utility because it is creative.

## Mudra

A seal or gesture — in yoga, a posture of the hands or body that expresses an inner state. Here: the posture of group discussion itself — openness that holds equivalence rather than coercion or sameness. The discussion or *vitarka* mudra expresses wholeness and equivalence in a gesture wherein the thumb and forefinger are joined while three remaining digits point outwards. Representing oneself, the other, and the discussion between or alternatively the mathematical symbol for equivalence (≡). The implication is to see one as an equivalent in an interaction which therefore demands due consideration.

## Omphalos

Greek: navel — the World Navel or axis mundi. In the Edshu myth: dynamic perspective that exposes the folly of mistaking one vantage for absolute truth. Here: the corrective to nickels-and-dimes certainty; the vantage from which protocols and custody of shared infrastructure become visible as one thing. The world possesses an intriguing but apparently random synchronicity with the Hindu Om and Lingam or Shivling (Phallus) which unites the unknowable with the fountainhead of *material* human creativity.

## Protocol

Use-case-specific public rules for how identity and value are registered and exchanged — the third arc after managed project and owned product. Here: how trust becomes plumbing; attested identity and transparent yield sharing one current across person, institution, and sovereign.

## Samadhi

Sanskrit समाधि: absorption — the state in which subject and scene are no longer held apart. Empathy and sensitivity are modulated and diminished as one becomes equanimous to the particular. Perhaps perceived as quietly insensitive the self is in a state of awe and reverence to the oneness of all. There is here the beginning of the distinction one makes between thingness and no thingness. Follows *dhyana*. This is the point at which the world looks back with at one new eyes. 

## Transition

The civilizational move from debased, opaque capital toward resilient architecture: identity made tenable on the social network, yield made legible, institutions earning loyalty through transparency. Follows *The Tenable*'s personal austerity into shared plumbing — less noise, more custody, time priced as if it mattered.

## Yoga Element

The *alignment* pillar of Dial Square — introspection and intuition gathered through introversion yokes the self to community and commons. Practiced as breath, posture, attention (*dharana*), meditation (*dhyana*), and absorption (*samadhi*), then taken off the mat into governance and service in our time of the Anthropocene. It demands an evolution theology - the idea of an ethic yoked to the basic elements of the natural sciences in a coherent and rational relationship.

## Yogasana

The physical postures of yoga practiced in traditions such as Sivananda, Hatha, Iyengar and Ashtanga designed to create openness and fluidity — blood flow, suppleness of tissue — as a basis for currency in the older sense: circulation and exchange within a living system. The risk is becoming identified with poses and to extend beyond what is reasonable and practical. These poses have at once been a corrective for the dysfunction of sedentary civilized life and as well performative expressions of ascetic renunciation of the forgetfulness implicit in householder life.

**Argonaut** is **JSON Intelligence**. It searches **Regnum Dei** — the published ontology of Transition Insight. Carta is the introductory essay, and the author’s shorthand for that whole; Argonaut is the voyage, not another name for the map.

The metaphor is Jason’s: seeking the golden fleece without fleecing anybody. The published corpus filters first. Optional open-web material is untrusted until it passes that filter. When the ontology cannot ground a claim, Argonaut defers and names the gap rather than inventing doctrine.

The live ask lives at `/argonaut`. The axioms remain Veritas, Utilitas, Firmitas; Soundness; Semper Idem; admit, defer, refuse.

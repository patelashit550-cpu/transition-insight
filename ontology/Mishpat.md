Mishpat (מִשְׁפָּט) is the **structural integrity and resilience** of an identity or system that has moved beyond what "just is" (Truth) to become what is "just" (Beauty) in society.  It refers to the administration of justice, including both legal decisions and ethical, compassionate actions toward the vulnerable, such as orphans, widows, and the poor. It implies upholding righteousness and restoring order. In the context of the **Identity Stack**, it is the distillation of a person’s stratified habits (**Tev**) into a legitimate, upright structure that reflects the divine image—turning a "mishmash" of heritage into a coherent, self-sovereign **Tel**, which rose stone by stone as an edifice representing who we are.

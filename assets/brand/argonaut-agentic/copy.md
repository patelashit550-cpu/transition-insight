## Summary (~100 words)

UBI may work like a reverse VAT. [A] VAT is essentially a payment to the commons for value added, meaning processing. [a] V≡RT runs the other way: a value equivalent reverse transfer [A] paid when a contribution in kind offsets a cost the commons would otherwise carry. Walking offsets the use of pharmacology; eating vegetables instead of refined carbohydrates or heavily processed foods does the same. [a] IoT factors using the ISO 20022 standard confirm the activity [a], an oracle prices it against what it displaces, and the income is streamed in stablecoin. It should self-fund. [A] It does need stablecoins to scale first. [a]
